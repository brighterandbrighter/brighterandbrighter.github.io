Privacy Policy

About this policy

This policy explains what personal information I collect and store when I am working with you, the reasons I collect it, what I do to I keep it safe, who (if anyone) can see it, how long I will keep it, and what your rights are with regards to your information. I've tried to write it as readably as I can, but by nature there is some complexity to it! So if anything is unclear, please ask me.

Who I am

I am Emma Winston (Brighter ADHD and Neurodiversity Coaching). I am a self-employed ADHD coach, practicing as a sole trader in the UK. For data protection purposes, I am the "data controller", which means I decide how your information is used and am responsible for looking after it.

  • Contact: brightercoach@gmail.com
  • ICO registration number: ZC304433

I practice in accordance with the Global Code of Ethics for coaches, mentors and supervisors, and the ICF Code of Ethics, both of which include commitments to confidentiality.

Information I collect

Contact details: your name, email address, and any other contact details.

Setup information: Anything you tell me in my setup forms. Other than your name and email, every question is optional. Some questions, such as any accessibility needs or other support you are receiving, might lead to you sharing health information if filled in. You are never required to, and this is indicated on the form.

Session notes: notes I make after our sessions to help me keep track of what we're working on and to support you as well as I can.

Anything you share in sessions and emails: information you choose to tell me during Zoom calls and in our email or WhatsApp conversations.

Payment and invoicing details: dates, payment records, and any other invoice information.

If your coaching is funded by Access to Work or an employer: session dates, fees, your name and the wording of your award on invoices, and any forms and signatures needed for funding claims if they are processed by post (discussed case by case).

I do not record our sessions myself, and I do not use Zoom's AI features myself such as meeting summaries, though if you wish to we may be able to discuss this on a case-by-case basis; however, if a feature you wish to use needs me to switch Zoom AI on account-wide, since I am unable to do this without compromising other clients' privacy, we will need to instead find a third-party solution which can be initiated by you (such as Otter on your own account for summarising). During periods of training I have sometimes been asked to record sessions, agreed with clients on an individual basis. This does not currently apply, but if, in the future, I ever needed to record a session for my own training, I would only do so with your signed consent, and you would always be free to say no.

Recordings or transcripts you make: if you'd like to record a session or use a transcript for your own use, including for accessibility, you are welcome to; I ask that you let me know you are doing this, if possible. You would start the recording on your side, and it would be stored on your device. I would not keep a copy. If you need to use Zoom to record rather than your own software, we would both receive a local copy of the recording, since I cannot turn off local recording as the host - I would immediately delete my copy after the call.

Health information

Health information is treated as "special category" data under UK law and needs extra care. I will only collect it if you have chosen to share it, and I will only use it to tailor our coaching to you. My setup form asks that you only enter health information if you consent to me holding it. You can withdraw that consent at any time and I will delete the information.

Your copy of notes, and shared documents

Session notes: At the end of our coaching together, I email you a copy of my session notes, so you have the same record I do. It can sometimes take me a while to transfer these notes across since I need to cross-check it to make sure nothing is missing, but once sent, your copy is yours to keep or delete as you wish.

The original local copy of my notes is stored under a pseudonym which only I know the reference to, in end-to-end encrypted software behind two passwords. I leave identifiable information (real names, employers, locations etcetera) out of these notes entirely.

Shared documents (optional): some clients like to keep shared notes in a tool they already use or that we try out during coaching. I offer this as a Google Doc by default, but this is entirely your choice. If you decide you would like to do this, either you can create it on your preferred platform in your own account, or, if you would like to use a Google doc and wish to use my standard template for this, as long as you have a Google account I will immediately transfer ownership of it to you after it is created. When our coaching ends, I remove my access, so you keep the document and I do not hold an extra copy other than the local version of any session notes which might have been copied into it. If you do not have a Google account, when we complete coaching I will email the Google doc to you as a .docx or PDF file and will then delete it from my own email sent box. Whatever tool you use for this, the information will be held by its provider under their terms.

Why I use your information, and the legal basis

  • To deliver coaching to you (in the setup session, when scheduling, in sessions themselves, in notes to keep track, follow-up emails). Basis: Delivering our contract as agreed.
  • To hold health information you choose to share. Basis: your explicit consent.
  • To invoice and keep financial records. Legal basis: legal obligation (HMRC record-keeping).
  • To process Access to Work or employer funding. Basis: Delivering our contract, and the funding arrangement you or your employer have agreed to.
  • To let you know when a coaching slot opens up (my mailing list). Basis: your consent. If you are on my mailing list, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or email me asking to be removed.

Who can see your information

Me. I am the only person who sees your intake form, session notes and health information.

My assistant. I work with one administrative assistant, based in the UK, who has signed a confidentiality agreement. Her name is Sam, and she can see client names, contact details and session times in my booking system. She only sees other information if I forward her a specific admin task, and I keep that to the minimum required - most admin is done by me and I will forward a message to Sam only if I cannot pick it up myself in a timely fashion. In the event of an emergency which means I cannot communicate with you, Sam may contact you on my behalf. I never share session content with her. If we mainly message on WhatsApp, I may ask your permission to add Sam to a group chat for scheduling and invoice reminders; you can always message me alone about anything more personal. Sam sometimes works while travelling, which means admin information could occasionally be accessed from outside the UK.

Employers or organisations funding your coaching. As standard, I only share the dates on which sessions took place and confirmation that you attended. If I cannot reach you after a cancellation or if you did not come to a session, I may contact your funding organisation to check whether you want to continue, but only after I have reached out to you three times first. I share anything else only with your explicit written permission. I never share what we discuss.

Access to Work (DWP). If your coaching is funded through Access to Work, invoices showing your name, session dates and rates, and claim forms with your name and signature, go to the DWP as part of the funding process.

Supervision. I receive regular supervision to support good coaching practice; this is required by EMCC though not by ICF, and I believe it is essential to delivering high-quality and safe coaching. During periods in which I am working towards a credential I may also work with an ICF mentor coach who is specifically trained in looking at my practice through a lens of the ICF competencies. If I discuss our work with my supervisor or mentor coach, I do so anonymously, without anything that could identify you. I only work with supervisors and mentor coaches who hold an active membership with a professional standards body using either the ICF code of ethics, or the Global Code of Ethics, and who are therefore bound by confidentiality themselves.

Talking to others on your behalf. If you'd like me to speak to someone else to get or share information, I'll ask you to give explicit written consent, and we'll agree between us exactly what I can share or ask them.

ICF and EMCC credentialling. Renewing my coaching credentials requires ICF and EMCC to verify my client hours. If you agree in my What To Expect document, I may share your name, email address and the start and end dates of our coaching with the ICF or EMCC for that purpose only. I don't ever share notes or session content, and you can say no without it affecting your work with me.

Testimonials and feedback (only if you agree). At the end of our work I may ask whether you'd like to write a short statement about your experience, or fill in a feedback form which may be submitted to EMCC towards my credential renewal. You're more than welcome to say no, and/or to be anonymous or pseudonymous if you say yes.

Legal requirements. In exceptional cases I might be legally required to share information, for example if there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else, or if required by a court. Where it is safe and lawful, I would always aim to talk to you about this first.

I will never sell your information or share it for marketing (ever).

My website

My website is a simple static site hand-built with Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not make use of cookies or analytics, and therefore collects significantly less of your information than most other sites on the internet! The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, meaning your browser connects to Google's servers and will share your IP address with Google when you visit; this is the case for all sites using Google Fonts. If you use the contact form at the bottom of my homepage, the name, email address and message you enter will be processed by Formspree, the form service I use, and will then be forwarded to my email. If you would prefer Formspree not to process your data, you can email me directly at brightercoach@gmail.com. GitHub, which hosts my website, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; you can read more in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Some of the above listed companies are based in, or store data in, the US or other countries outside the UK. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law. Zoom, Google, Squarespace (which runs Acuity) and Intuit (which runs Mailchimp and QuickBooks) take part in the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, while Formspree and GitHub rely on standard contractual clauses, and Apple uses a combination of the Data Privacy Framework and contractual safeguards. Tally stores data in the EU. My session notes are encrypted with my own password and biometric lock on any devices I use them on, including when they sync to iCloud.

AI usage and non-usage

Being transparent about AI usage and non-usage is personally important to me.

Where I do not use AI

I try very hard to ensure none of your information ever goes anywhere near any tool which could use it to train a large language model, or to otherwise send information to any AI company. I do not enter client names, contact details, session content, health information or anything else that could identify you into any cloud-based AI chatbot or other tool, ever.

I do not use generative AI to produce or edit content on my website, or in email or text contact. If you are reading something I have written, it was written by me and not by an LLM.

I keep AI features switched off on Zoom, but since Zoom sometimes re-enables its AI tooling after updates, I acknowledge it as the most significant risk to the above policy. Whenever I have run a Zoom update, I check settings to turn features back off manually if needed.

Where I use AI

I currently use AI in one specific use case relevant to this privacy policy:

I run a fully local, open-weight language model, Qwen3 (open-weight means that the values the LLM uses to generate output are made publicly available, rather than kept secret as they are in the major commercially available models), to read a downloaded copy of my emails daily and extract actionable dates and any verbs suggesting actions I might have missed, which are then written to a text file saved on an encrypted drive for me to read and cross-check against my real inbox. Qwen3 cannot learn from this data, has no access to anything outside the folder it runs in, cannot send information to any other people or companies, and has no ability to connect to the internet. No datacentre (other than the one Gmail's emails are already unavoidably stored in) is ever used in the process, and the LLM is powered entirely by my personal computer's hardware.

No AI company is ever given access to your information, and no AI is involved in our coaching work.

How I keep your information secure

  • Session notes are kept in an encrypted app which sits behind two passwords.
  • My devices are password-protected and use two-factor authentication on key accounts.
  • I delete setup forms from Tally when our work together ends.
  • My assistant only receives information needed for the specific task I have requested her to do, and I do not forward any non-administrative information.
  • I share as little information as possible with funders and others, and never session content.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the information I hold about you, including my session notes
  • correct any inaccuracies in that information
  • ask me to delete your information (unless I am legally required to keep it, for example in the case of financial records by HMRC)
  • restrict how I use it
  • withdraw consent to use of your information at any time, where I rely on consent
  • receive a copy of any information you've given me

If you would like to do any of these, please email me at brightercoach@gmail.com and I will respond within one month. There is no charge for doing so.

If something goes wrong

If a data breach happens that puts any of your information at risk, I will tell you as soon as possible, explain what happened and what I am going to do about it, and report it to the ICO where required.

Concerns and complaints

If you are unhappy with anything about how I have handled your information, please talk to me first and I will do my utmost to make it right. However, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

I review this policy at least once a year, and also when anything about my practice changes. It was last updated in November 2026.
